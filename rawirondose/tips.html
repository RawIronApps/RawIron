RAWIRON DOSE

Tips & Features

What RawIron Dose can do, and where to find it. Many of the most useful features are a swipe or a tap away.

Reminders Checking Off Doses Logging Other Doses History Notes Your Data

Reminders

Set up a reminder
On the Reminders tab, tap + and enter what you take: its name, medication or supplement, its form, and each dose (amount, unit, and strength). Add instructions like "with food" if you like.
Times and days
Give a reminder as many times a day as you need, and pick the days of the week (every day, weekdays, or the ones you choose), or every so many days, like every other day.
Courses that end
Turn on Ends and pick the last day for something you take for a while, like a 10-day antibiotic. It's dimmed under Reminders once it's over, and stays in your history.
Notifications
Each reminder can notify you at its times. A dose you've already checked off doesn't notify you. Turn on Remind Again to be notified every 5 to 60 minutes (up to 3 more times) until you check it off. Tap the bell on a reminder to turn its notifications on or off.
Rotate sites
For injections, topicals, patches, and drops, add the sites you rotate through under Site Rotation, like Left Quad and Right Quad. Each dose on Today shows the next site, and checking it off moves the next dose on to the site after it, then back to the first. Notifications and the Reminders tab show the next site too.

Checking Off Doses

Check it off
On Today, tap the circle beside a reminder's dose when you take it. Tap it again to mark it as not taken.
A different time or amount
Tap the dose itself instead of the circle to change the time you took it, how much, or to add a note or where it was injected or applied.
Skip a dose
Swipe left on a reminder's dose to skip it on purpose. Skipped doses are kept apart from missed ones.
Catch up on another day
Use ‹ and › beside the date to check off doses on a day you forgot. On a past day, a dose you check off is logged at its reminder time.

Logging Other Doses

Add any dose
Tap + on a part of the day, or the floating + button, to log anything you take that isn't on a reminder, like a pain reliever as needed.
Things you've taken before
Start typing a name and pick a suggestion: its type, form, and dose fill in from the last time you took it.
Sites you've used
Start typing where a dose was injected or applied and pick a suggestion from the sites you've used before. The ones you've used for that medication come first.
Any form
Tablets, capsules, liquids, injections, topicals, drops, inhalers, patches, powders, or anything else. The form sets the unit a dose starts with, and you can type any unit you like.
Take it again
Swipe right on a dose to copy it to today at the current time. On today, it logs another one now.

History

Your day at a glance
The top of Today shows how many reminder doses you've taken of those due, with how many you skipped, still have to take (or missed, on a past day), and your extra doses.
Every day
The History tab lists each day with the share of reminder doses you took and what you took, newest first. Days with a missed dose stand out. Tap a day to open it.
How far back
Choose how many past days History shows with the Days picker. The total at the top covers the days listed.
Every time you took it
On the Meds tab, pick a medication or supplement to see each dose, newest first, how often and how much you took, and its reminders. Swipe right on a dose to copy it to any day and time.

Notes

Notes about your day
On Today (or any day), tap + on the Notes card to write how the day went, like how you slept or how you felt. Tap a note to change or delete it.
Notes about a medication
On the Meds tab, pick a medication or supplement and tap Add note, for things like side effects or how well it's working. You can also choose Medication when adding a note from a day.
All your notes
The Notes tab lists every note, newest first. Tap a note to change it, swipe left to delete it, or swipe right to open its day. Tap + to add one.
Find a note
Show only Day Notes or Medication Notes. With Medication Notes, pick one medication or keep All Doses. On the Meds tab, See notes opens the Notes tab filtered to that medication.

Your Data

Dose journal
In Settings, tap Create next to Dose Journal and pick a date range. It starts with the reminder doses you took, every medication and supplement, and your reminders, then every day with each dose and its notes. Print it for your doctor, or save it as a PDF.
Back up your data
Export your doses, reminders, and notes to a file in Settings at any time, and restore them whenever you need to.
Private and offline
No account and no tracking: everything stays on your device and works without an internet connection.
Not medical advice
RawIron Dose helps you keep track. It doesn't check doses or interactions. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
