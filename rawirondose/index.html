DOSE. CHECK. REPEAT.

Stay on track, every day.

RawIron Dose keeps checking off quick and your history clear, so keeping up with your medications and supplements fits into your day instead of taking it over.

Reminders you check off

Set times and days for each medication or supplement—every day, chosen weekdays, or every few days—with an end day for a course. Get a notification, then check it off when you take it.

Remind again

Until a dose is checked off, be notified again every 5 to 60 minutes. A dose you've already taken never reminds you.

Log every dose

Tablets, capsules, liquids, injections, topicals, drops, inhalers, patches, and more. Log anything you take as needed, and skip a dose on purpose.

Rotate injection sites

Give a reminder the sites you rotate through, like left and right quad. Each dose shows the next site, and moves on once it's checked off.

Your meds, one tap away

Medications you've taken before are suggested as you type, and fill in their dose and form. Sites you've used are suggested too.

Notes on your days and meds

Write how a day went, or note side effects for a medication. The Notes tab shows them all, filtered by day notes or by medication.

Daily history

Every day with the share of reminder doses you took, what you took, and anything you missed. Tap a day for the details.

Every time you took it

Pick a medication or supplement to see each dose, how often and how much you took it, its reminders, and its notes.

Dose journal

Print your doses for your doctor or pharmacist, or save them as a PDF: adherence, every medication, your reminders, then every day with its doses and notes.

YOUR DATA

Designed to work offline.

RawIron Dose does not require an internet connection or an account to track your doses. Your medications, doses, reminders, and notes are kept on your device, and reminder notifications are scheduled on the device itself. Backup and restore tools are available in the app.

Internet access may be used when you choose to open external support, privacy, terms, or store-related pages.
