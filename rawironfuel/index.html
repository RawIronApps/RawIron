BUILT FOR THE WAY YOU EAT

Log every meal, fast.

RawIron Fuel keeps logging quick and the numbers clear, so tracking your nutrition fits into your day instead of taking it over.

Log every meal

Breakfast, lunch, and dinner, plus meals of your own like snacks or pre-workout, with servings and serving sizes. The floating + picks the meal for the time of day.

Your day at a glance

Calories, grams of protein, carbs, and fat for the day, with each macro's share of the calories and the totals of the values you track.

Track more than macros

Add iron, vitamin C, fiber, or anything else, with its unit. Each one is asked for per serving and totaled beside your macros.

Your foods, one tap away

Foods you've had before are suggested as you type, and fill in their serving size and macros when you pick one.

Repeat meals fast

Copy a meal from the last day you had it or any recent day, or swipe to copy any food to today.

Daily history

Every day you've logged with its totals and your daily average. Tap a day to see every meal and item.

Food history

Pick a food to see every time you had it, with its totals. Swipe to copy it to any day and meal.

Fuel journal

Print your food log or save it as a PDF: your totals, daily average, and most eaten foods, then every day with its meals.

Backups you control

Export your food log, tracked values, and meals to a file you keep, and restore it whenever you need to.

YOUR DATA

Designed to work offline.

RawIron Fuel does not require an internet connection, an account, or a food-database service to log your meals. Your food log is kept on your device, with backup and restore tools available in the app.

Internet access may be used when you choose to open external support, privacy, terms, or store-related pages.
