Common topics

How do I log a food?

On the Today tab, tap + on a meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner, or one of your own, like snacks), or use the floating + button to add to the meal for the time of day. Enter the name, servings, serving size, and the nutrition for one serving.

Does RawIron Fuel include a food database?

Not one of its own. Scan a package's barcode and its nutrition is looked up in Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central, or enter the nutrition yourself from the label. Once you've logged a food, it's suggested as you type, and picking it fills in its serving size and macros from the last time you had it.

How do I scan a barcode?

When adding a food, tap the barcode button next to the food name and hold the package's barcode inside the frame. The first time, allow RawIron Fuel to use the camera; if you turned that off, turn it back on in your device's Settings. You can also type the number printed under the barcode. Always check the values that fill in against the label before you save.

A barcode wasn't found. What now?

Not every product is in Open Food Facts or USDA FoodData Central yet. Enter the food from its label: RawIron Fuel remembers the barcode with it, so the next time you scan that package it fills in from your own history. If the app says it couldn't reach the food databases, check your internet connection and scan again.

Where does scanned nutrition come from?

From Open Food Facts, a free, collaborative food database whose data is available under the Open Database License, and from USDA FoodData Central (Branded Foods), U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service, when Open Food Facts doesn't have the product. These databases are maintained by others, and their values can be incomplete or out of date.

What if I don't know the calories?

Leave calories empty and RawIron Fuel works them out from the protein, carbs, and fat you enter, at 4, 4, and 9 kcal per gram.

Can I repeat a meal I've had before?

Yes. An empty meal offers to copy the same meal from the last day you had it, or from any recent day, and you can swipe right on any item from another day to copy it to today.

Does RawIron Fuel require an internet connection?

No internet connection is required for the app's food-logging functions. Looking up a scanned barcode needs a connection, unless you've scanned that food before. External links such as this support page open in your device's web browser when you choose to use them.

How do I back up my data?

Use the Your Data section in Settings to export your food log to a JSON file and to restore it. Keep important backup files in a location you control. You can also print your food log, or save it as a PDF, with Fuel Journal.

How do purchases work?

RawIron Fuel starts with a free 14-day trial, then a one-time lifetime purchase unlocks it—no subscription. Current pricing is shown on the App Store and Google Play, and purchases are processed through the applicable app store. Use Settings → License, or Restore Purchases on the Welcome page, when you need to restore an eligible purchase. Without a trial or license the app is read only, and you can still export your data.
