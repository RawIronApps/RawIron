Common topics

How do I log a food?

On the Today tab, tap + on a meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner, or one of your own, like snacks), or use the floating + button to add to the meal for the time of day. Enter the name, servings, serving size, and the nutrition for one serving.

Does RawIron Fuel include a food database?

No. You enter the nutrition for each food yourself, usually from its label. Once you've logged a food, it's suggested as you type, and picking it fills in its serving size and macros from the last time you had it.

What if I don't know the calories?

Leave calories empty and RawIron Fuel works them out from the protein, carbs, and fat you enter, at 4, 4, and 9 kcal per gram.

Can I repeat a meal I've had before?

Yes. An empty meal offers to copy the same meal from the last day you had it, or from any recent day, and you can swipe right on any item from another day to copy it to today.

Does RawIron Fuel require an internet connection?

No internet connection is required for the app's food-logging functions. External links such as this support page open in your device's web browser when you choose to use them.

How do I back up my data?

Use the Your Data section in Settings to export your food log to a JSON file and to restore it. Keep important backup files in a location you control. You can also print your food log, or save it as a PDF, with Fuel Journal.

How do purchases work?

RawIron Fuel starts with a free 14-day trial, then a one-time lifetime purchase unlocks it—no subscription. Purchases are processed through the applicable app store. Use Settings → License, or Restore Purchases on the Welcome page, when you need to restore an eligible purchase. Without a trial or license the app is read only, and you can still export your data.
