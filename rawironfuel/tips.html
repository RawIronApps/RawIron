RAWIRON FUEL

Tips & Features

What RawIron Fuel can do, and where to find it. Many of the most useful features are a swipe or a tap away.

Logging Food Your Day Repeat Meals History Your Foods Your Data

Logging Food

Add to a meal
Tap + on a meal to add a food or drink to it. The floating + button picks the meal for the time of day.
Foods you've had before
Start typing a name and pick a suggestion: its serving size and macros fill in from the last time you had it.
Calories from macros
Leave kcal empty and it's worked out from the protein, carbs, and fat (4, 4, and 9 kcal per gram).
Servings
Enter the nutrition for one serving, then how many servings you had. The totals multiply for you.
Track more than macros
Add values like iron, vitamin C, or fiber with their unit (mg, mcg, g) under Settings → Tracked Values. Each one is asked for per serving when you add a food, beside the macros.

Your Day

Totals and macro split
The top of the day shows your calories, grams of protein, carbs, and fat, and each macro's share of the calories, and the day's total of each value you track.
Move between days
Use ‹ and › beside the date to see other days, and Go to today to come back. You can plan up to tomorrow.
Edit or delete
Tap an item to change it or move it to another meal. Swipe left on it to delete it.

Repeat Meals

Copy a meal
An empty meal offers to copy the same meal from the last day you had it, in one tap.
Copy from an older day
An empty meal also offers Copy from an older day…: pick any recent day's meal, with its foods and totals, to copy. The Days picker sets how far back it looks.
Copy a food to today
Swipe right on an item from another day to copy it to today. On today, it duplicates the item.

History

Every day you've logged
The History tab lists each day with its calories, macros, and tracked values, newest first, with your daily average.
See a day's details
Tap a day to open it: every meal and item, ready to edit or copy.
How far back
Choose how many past days History shows with the Days picker.

Your Foods

Every time you had a food
On the Foods tab, pick a food to see each time you had it, newest first, with its totals and average calories. Tap one to open that day.
Copy it to any day
On the Foods tab, swipe right on a time you had a food to copy it to any day and meal.

Your Data

Fuel journal
In Settings, tap Create next to Fuel Journal and pick a date range. It starts with your totals, daily average and most eaten foods, then every day with its meals, including the values you track. Print it, or save it as a PDF.
Back up your data
Export your food log, including your tracked values, to a file in Settings at any time, and restore it whenever you need to.
Private and offline
No account and no tracking: everything stays on your device and works without an internet connection.

Still have a question?

The in-app version of this guide is in Settings → About & Support → Tips & Features. For anything else, visit Support.
