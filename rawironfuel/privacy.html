RAWIRON FUEL

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

In short: RawIron Fuel is designed to keep your food log on your device. The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its core food-logging features.

1. Information you enter into the app

RawIron Fuel lets you log the foods and drinks you have at each meal, with their servings, serving sizes, and nutrition (calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat). This information is stored locally by the app on your device so that you can use the app and maintain your food history.

2. Personal information

RawIron Fuel does not require an account, name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to use its core features. We do not intentionally collect your food log for our own servers or maintain a user profile for you.

3. Internet access

The core food-logging functions of RawIron Fuel are designed to work without an internet connection. The app may open external web pages when you choose to access support, privacy, terms, or other linked resources. Visiting those external resources is subject to the policies of the services that host them.

4. Purchases and licensing

RawIron Fuel may offer a paid lifetime entitlement or other purchase options. Purchases are processed through the applicable platform's store, such as Apple App Store or Google Play. The platform may process information necessary to complete and manage the transaction under its own privacy policies. RawIron Fuel does not receive your payment-card information.

5. Backup and restore

RawIron Fuel provides backup and restore functionality for your food log, and a Fuel Journal you can print or save as a PDF. When you export, print, or share a backup or journal, the resulting file or printout is handled by you and by any operating-system or third-party service you choose to use for that file. We do not receive a copy merely because you create or restore a backup or create a journal.

6. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

RawIron Fuel is not designed around advertising or behavioral tracking. We do not use your food history to build an advertising profile.

7. Children's privacy

RawIron Fuel is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the app.

8. Third-party services

The mobile platform and its app-store services may collect or process information independently of RawIron Fuel. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

9. Data security

No software or device-storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use the device security and backup practices appropriate for your circumstances.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
